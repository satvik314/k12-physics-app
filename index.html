How every lab works

Predict. Play. Prove.

1

Predict

Before anything moves, students commit to a guess. Being wrong out loud is where the learning starts.

2

Play

Drag, launch, tune. Every lab is a real simulation that reacts instantly, with the numbers shown live.

3

Prove

Each lab ends with a challenge you can only pass if you understood the idea. Only then do we show the formula.

Lab 01 · Mechanics · ~6 min

Projectile Motion

Why does a thrown ball curve? Split its motion into two simple pieces, catch it at the top of its flight, then use what you learned to hit a target.

Lab 02 · Energy · ~6 min

Conservation of Energy

Energy never disappears, it only changes form. Drop a skater into a half-pipe, find out what it takes to clear a hill, and see where the energy goes when friction shows up.

Lab 03 · Waves · ~7 min

Waves & Superposition

Two waves in the same place simply add. From that one rule come loud spots, silent spots, beats, standing waves and the noise-cancelling headphones you build at the end.
